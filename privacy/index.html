MYSTERY SOLVE

Your case files stay yours.

Mystery Solve is a fictional detective game. The characters, messages, photographs, and locations presented as evidence belong to its story.

Account information

When account services are connected, Supabase Auth manages your email address, password authentication, account identifier, and sign-in session. The game stores your detective name, case progress, collected clue identifiers, notebook text, and save timestamps. It does not store readable passwords in game records.

Saved investigations

Solo game records are restricted to their owner through database row-level security. Co-op room members share collected clues, team notes, detective names, and solved status. Anyone you give the invite code can join until the room reaches six members; share it only with intended teammates. Guest progress uses this tab’s session storage and does not provide cross-device saving. A guest session is not a cloud account.

Browser storage

The site uses session storage for guest play and authentication session storage when you sign in. It does not include advertising trackers. Fonts and account services may make requests to their respective providers.

Prototype status

Mystery Solve is a public game connected to its Supabase project. Account and investigation data use the protections described above. The owner database view is restricted to an authorized, confirmed account.
