MYSTERY SOLVE

Trust the clues. Question the story.

Noor’s phone is open. Your task is to find where she goes and who she trusts. This is a fictional, self-contained investigation; every clue you need is in the game.

1. Explore the phone

Read conversations in Messages, inspect dates and details in Photos, and follow the saved route in Maps. Notes and voicemail fill in the missing pieces. Use the bar at the bottom to return to the phone’s home screen.

2. Build your case

Select “Pin to evidence” when you find something that matters. Open a person’s photograph on the corkboard to see their profile. A locked note uses a four-digit date in day-month order; a conversation and a photo give you the date.

3. Find Noor

Collect at least five clues, including Sara’s message, the saved route, and the private note. Then select “Solve the case” and choose a destination and a trusted contact. Incorrect answers let you continue investigating.

Solve it together

Open Play together, sign in, and create a private room. Share its invite code with up to five friends. Everyone explores their own phone view while pinned clues, hints, the ending, and team notes sync across the room. Room investigations stay in Play together for everyone to resume.

Keep your progress

A guest investigation remains in the current browser tab. When Supabase is connected, signing in saves each investigation, its evidence, and your notebook to your account. My investigations lets you resume a case or start a fresh attempt.
